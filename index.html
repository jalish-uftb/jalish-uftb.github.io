Glove Z

2025ESP32, MAX30102, DHT22, AD8232, Flex sensors, Blynk, ThingSpeak, Flask, Twilio

A wearable health monitor for bedridden patients that also lets them signal their needs with hand gestures.

  • Reads heart rate, SpO2, temperature and ECG on an ESP32.
  • Shows data live in Blynk, logs it to ThingSpeak, and sends WhatsApp alerts to caregivers through Twilio.
  • Six machine learning models classify Healthy, Warning and Critical states. A tuned Random Forest reached 96.8% test accuracy on a 60,000-sample synthetic dataset.
